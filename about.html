OUR HERITAGE & PROMISE

Connecting Purley, Surrey & London with Total Reliability

Station Cars Purley was founded with a singular mission: provide Croydon, Purley, Coulsdon, and surrounding Surrey areas with an uncompromisingly punctual, trustworthy, and premium minicab service.

Headquartered directly at 10 Station Approach, Purley, our central hub operates around the clock. Whether you are catching a dawn flight from Gatwick or Heathrow, traveling to a crucial board meeting in Central London, or returning safely home late at night, our courteous drivers and modern fleet ensure a stress-free experience every single time.

Every vehicle in our fleet is fully licensed, regularly sanitized, and maintained to manufacturer standards. Unlike app-based surge platforms, our quotes are fixed, honest, and competitive.
